How this works

This tool runs entirely in your browser. It splits your text into sentences and words to estimate how hard it is to read.

The scores are estimates based on word length and sentence length, not judgments of quality.

  • Flesch Reading Ease: Scores out of 100. Higher is easier.
  • Flesch-Kincaid Grade: Estimates the US school grade needed to understand the text.
  • Gunning Fog: Measures complex words (3 or more syllables).
  • SMOG: Also measures polysyllables, needs at least 30 sentences.
  • Coleman-Liau: Uses letter counting instead of syllables.
  • ARI: Uses character and word counts.
